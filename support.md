# TCGLedgr Support

Need help with TCGLedgr, or found a bug? Email **support@tcgledgr.com** and we'll
get back to you.

When reporting an issue, it helps to include:

- What you were trying to do
- What happened instead
- Your device model and iOS version (Settings → General → About)
