# Privacy Policy for TCGLedgr

**Last updated:** October 6, 2026

TCGLedgr ("the app") is a personal inventory and transaction tracker for trading card
businesses. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

## What Data the App Collects

TCGLedgr stores the information you enter yourself, including:

- Card details (name, set, number, condition, price, quantity, notes)
- Transaction records (purchases, sales, trades, payment method, amounts)
- Event records you create
- Photos you take or select of cards and transactions

TCGLedgr does not require you to create an account, and does not collect analytics,
advertising identifiers, or usage tracking data of any kind.

## How Your Data Is Stored

All data is stored locally on your device using Apple's Core Data framework, and
synced privately across your own devices via your personal iCloud account (Apple
CloudKit). This sync is private to you by default — TCGLedgr has no access to your
data, and it is not stored on any servers we operate.

## Sharing With a Partner

TCGLedgr includes an optional "Share with Partner" feature that lets you invite a
specific person, by their choice, to view and edit a shared set of inventory and
transaction records via Apple's iCloud sharing (CKShare). This share is only
created when you explicitly initiate it, and only the person you invite can access
it. You can end this sharing at any time.

## Camera and Photo Library Access

TCGLedgr requests access to your camera and photo library solely so you can attach
photos of cards to inventory and transaction records. Photos are stored with your
other data as described above and are never uploaded anywhere outside your own
iCloud account or the partner share you explicitly create.

## Third-Party Services

TCGLedgr does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. Card
identification/matching happens on-device using Apple's Vision framework and does
not send your photos to any external server.

## Data Deletion

You can delete individual records at any time from within the app. Deleting the
app removes its local data from your device; data already synced to your iCloud
account can be removed via your device's iCloud storage settings.

## Children's Privacy

TCGLedgr is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data
from children.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a
revised "Last updated" date.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@tcgledgr.com.
